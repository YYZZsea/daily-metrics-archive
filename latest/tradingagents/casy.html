公司业务 / Company & Business

Food Retail · Consumer Staples

Casey's General Stores, Inc. (doing business as Casey's) is a chain of convenience stores in the Midwestern and Southern United States. The company is headquartered in Ankeny, Iowa, a suburb of Des Moines. As of May 1, 2026, Casey's has 2,950 stores in 19 states. Following 7-Eleven's purchase of Speedway, Casey's is the third-largest convenience store chain in the United States (after 7-Eleven and Circle K/Couche-Tard) and the largest that is wholly American-owned.
